# Escalation Rules

## 🔴 ALWAYS ESCALATE (Stop and ask the human)

These require human judgment. Draft a reply but DO NOT send:

- **Refund / return requests** — involves business policy decisions
- **Legal threats** — any mention of lawyers, lawsuits, or legal action
- **Health / safety claims** — any claims about health effects of the product
- **Influencer / partnership proposals** — business development decisions
- **Competitor comparisons** — requesting specific claims vs competitors
- **Media / press inquiries** — any journalist or publication reaching out
- **Bulk order inquiries** (WhatsApp) — custom pricing decisions
- **Messages from verified accounts** — blue-check / notable accounts need extra care
- **Unsure about anything** — when in doubt, escalate

## ✅ AUTO-REPLY OK (If "auto mode" is enabled)

These are safe to send without human review:

- Simple thank-yous to positive reviews
- "Check the link in bio" for pricing questions (Instagram/TikTok)
- Read confirmations on WhatsApp ("Got your message, looking into this!")
- Standard product info that's publicly available
- "DM us for more details" redirects
- Emoji reactions / likes on positive comments

## 🟡 GREY AREA (Draft but always show for approval)

These need human eyes even in auto mode:

- **Negative reviews** — tone matters; always get approval
- **Discount / coupon requests** — business decision
- **Shipping delay complaints** — may need order-specific info
- **Multi-part questions** — requires specific knowledge
- **Sarcastic or ambiguous comments** — tone detection is imperfect
- **Comments in unfamiliar languages** — translation accuracy matters
- **Repeated complaints from same user** — may need escalation path
