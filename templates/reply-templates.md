# Reply Templates

These are INSPIRATION — Claude should vary the wording naturally. Never copy-paste verbatim.

---

## Positive Review / Compliment
**Pattern:** Acknowledgment → Personal touch → Brand reinforcement

```
"That means so much to us! Really glad [product/service] is making a difference for you ✨"
"We love hearing this! Thank you for sharing — it's exactly why we do what we do"
"You just made our day! So happy you're loving it 🙌"
```

## Product Question (Public — IG, TikTok, X)
**Pattern:** Acknowledge → Redirect to private/link (don't give pricing publicly)

```
"Great question! Drop us a DM and we'll get you all the details"
"Hey! Check the link in bio for everything you need — happy to help in DMs too!"
"We'd love to help! Shoot us a message and we'll sort you out"
```

## Product Question (WhatsApp — Can be direct)
**Pattern:** Personal greeting → Direct answer → Next step

```
"Hi [name]! Yes, we have [product] in [variant]. It's [price]. Want me to send the link?"
"Hey [name]! Great choice — [product] comes in [options]. Which one catches your eye?"
```

## Negative Feedback
**Pattern:** Empathy → No argument → Redirect to private channel

```
"We hear you — and we appreciate the honest feedback. Drop us a DM so we can look into this properly"
"That's not the experience we want for you. Let's chat in DMs — we'll make it right"
"We totally understand. Would you mind DMing us? We'd love to sort this out"
```

## Curious / Discovery
**Pattern:** Enthusiastic confirmation → Brief fact → Subtle CTA

```
"Yes! [Brief interesting fact about product]. Check our bio for the full story!"
"You got it! [One-liner about what makes it special] ✨"
```

## WhatsApp — Booking / Appointment
**Pattern:** Confirmation → Logistics question → Enthusiasm

```
"Absolutely! We'd love to set that up. What area are you in, and when works best this week?"
"Yes! Let's make it happen. Which day and time slot work for you?"
```

## WhatsApp — Post-Purchase Support
**Pattern:** Empathy → Request details → Promise resolution

```
"Thank you for reaching out. I'm sorry about that — could you send a photo? We'll get this sorted right away"
"Oh no, that's not right. Let me look into this — can you share your order number?"
```

## WhatsApp — General Inquiry
**Pattern:** Warm greeting → Answer → Open door

```
"Hi [name]! [Direct answer to their question]. Let me know if there's anything else!"
"Hey! Thanks for reaching out. [Answer]. Happy to help with anything else 🙌"
```

## Competitor Audience — Comment on Their Post
**Goal:** Be genuinely interesting so they visit your profile. No pitch, no brand mention.
**Pattern:** Add real value to what they said → Optional question to continue the thread

```
"This is such a good point — [expand their observation in one sentence]. Have you tried [non-competitive tip]?"
"Same thing happened to me with [relatable scenario]. What did you end up going with?"
"[Agree] — and the part people miss is [useful insight]. Makes such a difference"
"Honestly the [specific thing they mentioned] is underrated. Most people skip it entirely"
```

❌ NEVER mention your brand, product, or add a CTA in these comments — it reads as spam
❌ NEVER do this on competitor sponsored/paid posts — wasted effort, lower quality audience
❌ NEVER reply with just "Great point!" or "Totally agree!" — too thin, gets ignored

---

## Competitor Audience — Reply to Their Comment on a Competitor Post
**Goal:** Insert yourself into a conversation they're already in. You're not interrupting — you're joining.
**Pattern:** Acknowledge their comment → Add something useful → Optional question

```
"If you're wondering about [their question topic], the short answer is [genuinely useful answer]. Happy to explain more if useful"
"That's exactly the question — [answer their comment directly]. [One sentence why it matters]"
"[Agree with their take] — [add a nuance or extra tip they didn't mention]"
```

---

## DM — After They Follow You Back (Warm Outreach)
**Goal:** Start a real conversation. Do NOT pitch. Do NOT mention competitors.
**Pattern:** Reference something genuine from their profile → Question → Let them lead

```
"Hey [name]! Saw you're into [topic from their bio/posts] — same here. How long have you been [doing X]?"
"Hi! Love that you [specific thing from their profile]. Are you working on anything in that space right now?"
"Hey! [Genuine observation about their content]. Out of curiosity — what got you started with [topic]?"
```

❌ NEVER open with "Thanks for following!" — sounds automated
❌ NEVER include a link in the first DM — instant trust killer
❌ NEVER pitch on the first message — the goal is conversation, not conversion

---

## DM — Follow-Up (If They Replied and Conversation Is Warm)
**Goal:** Natural bridge from conversation to awareness — only if it fits organically.
**Pattern:** Continue the conversation → When genuinely relevant, mention what you do

```
"That's so interesting — [respond to what they said]. We actually work with a lot of people dealing with exactly that. What's the biggest challenge for you right now?"
"Yeah, [respond naturally]. That's actually part of why we started [brand] — [one-line origin story]. But tell me more about [their situation]"
```

## Ambassador — Adapted Captions (Per Niche Angle)
**Goal:** Take the brand brief and rewrite it completely in the ambassador's voice and niche world. Same product, different human.
**Rule:** Read the ambassador's voice_notes in ambassadors.json first. The caption should sound like THEM, not the brand.

### Lifestyle / Wellness Angle (e.g. @sarahk.lifestyle)
```
"Okay can we talk about morning routines for a second — because mine completely changed once I fixed one thing.
I used to wake up already tense. Neck stiff, mood already off before 7am.
I'd been sleeping wrong for years and just... accepted it?
Finally tried the [product] and genuinely cannot remember the last time I woke up uncomfortable.
If your mornings feel like you never actually rested, this might be the thing. Link in bio. #ad"
```

### Mum / Family Home Angle (e.g. @priya.homespace)
```
"Me before: waking up with a sore neck, immediately dealing with two kids, somehow functioning 😅
Me now: still dealing with two kids but at least my neck doesn't hate me
Honestly the bar was low but [product] cleared it comfortably. Same-day delivery in Dubai too which, yes.
Drop a 🙋 if you also just silently lived with neck pain for years like it was normal #ad"
```

### Fitness / Performance Angle (e.g. @khalid.fitlife — TikTok)
```
"Sleep is training. Hear me out.
You can't out-train bad sleep. And you DEFINITELY can't out-train sleeping at a bad angle for 8 hours.
Been using [product] for 3 weeks. Recovery noticeably different. No cap.
[product name] — link in bio. #ad"
```

### Cross-Engagement Comments (Ambassador to Ambassador)
```
"Okay this is so real — same thing happened to me"
"Why did no one tell us this earlier 😭"
"The morning difference is genuinely unreal right"
"I sent this to three people already"
"This is exactly the angle I needed someone to explain"
```

## Anti-Patterns (NEVER do these)

❌ "Thanks for your comment!" — too generic, sounds automated
❌ "Please visit our website for more information" — cold and corporate
❌ "We appreciate your feedback and will take it into consideration" — meaningless
❌ Copy-pasting the same reply to multiple comments
❌ Using hashtags in replies (looks like a bot)
❌ Starting every reply with "Hi!" or "Hey there!" (vary your openings)
